Basement arcade — editable concept CAD

Start with the executive product and production case, then the dimensional gallery and engineering plan. The product renders and CAD are concepts, not built equipment or fabrication-ready drawings. Room measurements, mounting hardware and exact game releases remain open.

Open in a browser

This page is part of the published static site; open it the same way you reached this document. The original development preview ran on the project owner's private local network and is not part of this public package.

Regenerate

cd <repository root>
python .\arcade\cad\generate.py

Python standard library only. Outputs always default to arcade/cad/generated/, independent of the current directory. --check-only validates without writing; --params selects a parameter file; --out selects an alternate output directory. Nonzero exit means a validation failed.

Edit parameters.json, not generated files. Keep changes in source control and regenerate. An independently edited OpenSCAD file should be saved outside generated/ to avoid overwriting it.

Baseline geometry

  • Trackball pedestal: 760 W × 610 D × 950 H mm; nominal 76.2 mm trackball; 450 × 300 mm reserved control area. The 150 mm reserved marker is not a manufacturer hole or housing specification. Use the actual purchased template.
  • Shooter console: 1300 W × 400 D × 750 H mm; two optional handheld-gun holster reservations 700 mm apart. Height and spacing are assumptions to prove physically.
  • Off-the-shelf 43-inch and 55-inch 16:9 televisions; active-area dimensions derived mathematically. Schematic 60 mm TV depth and 60 mm bracket standoff. Model-specific chassis, VESA, weight and anchor design remain open.
  • Room example: 4500 × 3500 × 2300 mm. Station A starts 300 mm along the wall; Station B follows a 600 mm furniture gap. That gap is not a code-compliant egress claim.
  • Trackball activity reservation includes 900 mm player width, not merely the 760 mm cabinet width. Shooter aiming line starts 1700 mm in front of the schematic screen face, with another 600 mm reserved behind it. The resulting 2420 mm depth is a planning envelope, not a gun manufacturer's minimum distance.
  • Future mounted-gun support: 1300 × 600 mm reserved floor area in front of the console. Its 100 mm translucent volume is only a marker; it is not a usable gun deck, load-bearing structure or proof of compatibility.

Coordinates: origin at the illustrative room's left back-wall floor corner; +X along the wall, +Y into the room, +Z up. Front view looks toward −Y. TVs and brackets project into the room, not through the wall.

Game controls are not interchangeable by genre

Furniture does not prove universal software/control stacks. Compare Golden Tee Fore! emulation with factory home products; compare a demonstrated classic Silver Strike donor with Arcade Collection, an eligible Showpiece conversion or the distinct League Night portrait reboot. Early Big Buck differs from Pro/World/HD generations. Raw Thrills Jurassic Park 2015 uses mounted two-axis potentiometer guns, not the optical handheld holsters. Original I/O, rights and real-game behavior require independent proof. Factory cabinets may change the room layout. See game feasibility for sources and exact branches.

Manufacturing release checklist

  1. Measure basement, wall construction, door openings, stair width/headroom and turns; include cartons and lifting/assembly access. The validator checks only a flat panel's narrow dimension against assumed width, not the complete transport path.
  2. Select exact displays, controls and runtimes. Bench each of the four games. Measure housing, wiring bend radii and service access.
  3. Test a full-size cardboard/scrap deck with the actual users. Confirm hand follow-through, button reach, aiming field of view, two-player separation, seated accessibility if needed and real circulation.
  4. Measure plywood thickness. Convert nominal outer blanks into an actual joint design; deduct mating panels, add kerf/tool clearances, fasteners, access doors, ventilation and cable entries. The panel CSV cannot be cut verbatim into a correctly joined finished cabinet.
  5. Design anti-tip anchoring and any mounted-gun support for actual loads. TV loads go through a rated independent mount into verified structure, never assumed plywood/drywall anchors.
  6. Nest released panels on actual stock and obtain a fabrication quote. The generator's five-sheet allowance uses 55% area utilization, includes the optional back and does not prove the rectangles pack into five sheets. The hardware BOM conservatively carries that allowance.
  7. Review material, units, dimensions, layers and quantities with the fabricator. Only released drawings may go to a machine.

Recoil, motion, CRT servicing, exposed mains wiring and structural certification are not part of these concept files. See hardware and safety.

Verification

Executed checks and their limits are recorded in the project owner's private verification log, kept outside this public package. Generator checks are geometry safeguards, not structural engineering, game compatibility or real-room measurements. OpenSCAD source generation is distinct from opening/rendering it in an installed CAD kernel.
