Arcade Studio — executive rebuild brief

Owner and mandate

Personal-domain continuation of Mike's basement arcade project. A production/business case is a scenario for this personally owned concept, not authorization to create a company, publish, contact vendors, buy parts or move payload into another domain. Specialists are AI advisory/implementation workers, not retained credentialed human consultants.

Mike rejected the previous output as shallow and visually poor. Required outcomes: 1. Arcade/video-game-design advisor reviews the actual proposal and fixes its substance. 2. Business/product-marketing advisor reviews and fixes positioning and executive presentation. 3. Show finished-product concept visuals and the player/game experience, not just cabinet rectangles. 4. Present the general product and production business plan in executive framing. 5. Distribute independent work in parallel; one integration owner and evidence-based acceptance.

Audience, job and recommendation to test

Audience: Mike as prospective owner/founder deciding whether to fund a compelling prototype and later a small production pilot. Job: decide the product, fidelity, spend and evidence needed next. Do not sell a fictitious finished product.

Working concept name: AFTER HOURS / Arcade Studio (private working label; not trademark-cleared). Initial hypothesis: two complementary furniture/control stations create more authentic play and better serviceability than one compromised universal cabinet, without four commercial shells. Advisors must challenge this, not simply agree. Four targets remain Golden Tee, Big Buck Hunter, Silver Strike and Jurassic Park Arcade. Exact releases remain unselected; do not silently replace them.

Commercial proposal must be separate from personal one-off construction. Hardware-only sales, authorized donor integration and a licensed turnkey product have different legal, support and economic models. Pick a plausible initial offer and explain what customers do NOT receive. No packaged unlicensed game binaries, artwork, keys, dongle bypass or false affiliation.

Evidence already available

Read relevant sections, not every file wholesale: - ../PLAN.md — current technical plan, not an executive narrative. - ../research/game-feasibility.md — curated primary-source version/control evidence. - ../research/hardware-and-fabrication.md — current prototype infrastructure assumptions and observed supplier prices. - ../research/rea-workflow.md — actual REA tool smoke is NOT a game test. - ../cad/parameters.json — baseline nominal geometry. - The project owner's private verification record — what was exercised versus not (not included in this public package).

Known source-backed constraints: early Golden Tee Fore and Eagle-era Big Buck are emulation candidates; Silver Strike has no proven open-source runtime in this work; Raw Thrills Jurassic 2015 uses mounted potentiometer guns and separate original I/O/security hardware. Older Sega Jurassic titles are different games. Source flags do not prove speed or a lawful commercial bundle.

Shared product / visual contracts

Nominal outer dimensions unless the arcade advisor provides a justified revision: - Trackball: 760 W x 610 D x 950 H mm; 76.2 mm ball; credible clear hand-sweep space and unobtrusive game/menu controls. - Shooter: 1300 W x 400 D x 750 H mm low furniture; 55-inch independently supported display; two handheld holsters OR a visibly distinct mounted-control configuration. - Trackball display: 43-inch independent screen. Illustrative room: 4500 x 3500 mm, not Mike's measured room. - Finishes: warm walnut/wood accents, matte charcoal structure, restrained amber/copper lighting, teal status details. Premium residential furniture, not a box covered in RGB stripes. - Render actual depth, bevels, controls, screen surfaces, speaker/vent/service details, material/shadow hierarchy and room context. Concept renders must be labelled as such. No claim of a built or photographed product. - On-screen golf/bowling/wildlife/jungle scenes must be original illustrative artwork, NOT counterfeit game screenshots. Each caption says that actual target-game appearance and support depend on the selected lawful release. - Draw at least two strong finished-product views, a room/context view, a mounted-gun alternative, control close-ups and four player-experience vignettes. Better one excellent hero than ten technical wireframes. - Five creative directions must be compared, then select one coherent direction; do not average all five into blandness.

Ownership and output interfaces

One owner per slice; do not edit another slice's files. All workers MUST skip builds, tests, linters, formatters and browser rendering mid-flight. Integration owner runs the final renders, executable economics checks and X1 browser acceptance once assembled. Source research and ordinary data calculations are allowed. Message the parent promptly with blocking findings; do not wait for another worker if your slice is independent.

Arcade / game-experience advisor-worker

Own reviews/arcade-review.md, experience-spec.md, experience.json. - Audit experience, controls, runtime architecture, authentic versus acceptable substitutions, ergonomic assumptions and feasibility sequencing. - Write ranked defects with location, expected/observed consequence and concrete correction; then implement those corrections in your specification and JSON. - Provide the full guest journey (power-on through selection, calibration, multiplayer, session end, service/recovery), four game-specific play rituals and control requirements, sensing/latency budgets, runtime decision tree, and prototype acceptance tests. - experience.json: {recommendation, corrections:[...], games:[{id,title,desiredExperience,physicalActions,controlContract,softwareBranches,fidelityRisks,prototypeTest,visualBrief}], journey:[...], architectureDecisions:[...], sources:[{id,title,url,observedOn,claim}]}. Plain JSON, meaningful prose values, no invented test outcomes.

Business / product-marketing advisor-worker

Own reviews/marketing-review.md, narrative.json. - Review why the old presentation failed, define differentiated positioning, target buyer, product promise, exclusions, objections and launch narrative. - Compare five distinct executive creative directions. Recommend one product-launch/decision-memo direction with disciplined evidence and commercial consequences. - Current competitor benchmarks with source/date/configuration, no fabricated market size or customer validation. No need to force a weak TAM number. - narrative.json: {positioning, audience, thesis, thirtySecondPitch, selectedDirection, directions:[...], chapters:[{id,headline,point,evidenceNeeded,visual}], competitors:[{name,configuration,priceUsd,priceBasis,url,observedOn,comparisonLimit}], objections:[...], validationPlan:[...], sources:[...]}. - User-facing copy must be concise, specific and suitable for an executive, not a developer tool report. Provide recommended corrected text, not just criticism.

Industrial / game-experience visual designer-worker

Own ALL of visuals/. No ownership of the final presentation HTML. - Produce editable 3D scene source and original screen artwork. Prefer a local Three.js scene with all dependencies vendored under visuals/ and a deterministic view/capture interface. Blender is not on PATH; Node and Python are available. Do not spend the assignment installing a large DCC tool. - Create visuals/render.html plus scene source and assets, with ?view=hero|room|trackball|shooter|mounted|exploded selecting deterministic camera/scene. Expose window.renderReady=true only after textures/assets and at least one finished frame. No continuous animation needed. Include a documented button/function to change views. - Parent will run browser captures into visuals/hero.png, room.png, trackball.png, shooter.png, mounted.png, exploded.png. If WebGL is unavailable, give a genuine alternate renderer, not a blank or fake fallback; notify parent before changing interface. - Provide four original game-experience illustrations under visuals/experience-{golf,bowling,hunt,jungle}.svg or PNG, depicting gameplay composition and meaningful interaction cues. Add a source/capture manifest and clear design limitations. - No internet dependencies at viewing time, no copyrighted logos, no fake photographs. Do not run browser/build/render checks; parent owns those after integration.

Production / unit-economics advisor-worker

Own production-plan.md, economics.json, economics.py, economics.csv. - Develop a real make/buy, DFM, supplier/assembly/test/packaging/service process, pilot plan, quality/compliance/liability gates, funding and go/no-go case. - Model prototype plus 10/50/100-unit production scenarios; distinguish batch size from annual demand and demand hypotheses from forecasts. Select a credible initial SKU/offer instead of assuming four licensed games come free. - economics.json is the numeric source of truth: currency, product scope/exclusions, scenarios with id/volume/targetPriceUsd and named per-unit cost lines, variable selling/support costs, fixed launch costs, assumptions and evidence references. economics.py computes unit gross/contribution margins, break-even and working-capital needs, writes economics.csv, and supports a clear price/cost sensitivity query. Standard library only; parent runs it. Keep license/donor costs explicit when unknown; hardware-only scope may exclude them, turnkey may NOT silently assign zero. - Include landed costs, labor, packaging/freight treatment, warranty/returns reserve, channel fees, CAC assumptions, tooling/NRE, inventory/deposit timing, and sensitivity/stop conditions. Distinguish estimates from observed quotes; do not fabricate supplier discounts.

Integration owner (parent)

Own this brief, index.html, final CSS/JS, source linking, changes to old entry points, presentation QA, cross-host delivery and synthesis. Build a strong visual narrative while workers run. Retain engineering appendices but do not make them the executive front door. Update the project graph and decision gates to reflect the real product/business work.

Release acceptance

  • Every requested advisor/review/output exists; the presentation incorporates substantive corrections.
  • Executive can understand product, experience, build path, commercial offer, economics and decision in a five-minute read.
  • Finished-product imagery dominates the opening; original experience scenes are present for all four target games.
  • Clear distinction among observed evidence, design proposal, estimates and unproven game support. No invented photos, market validation, certifications or compatibility.
  • Economics reconcile programmatically; 10/50/100-unit assumptions and unknown rights costs are visible.
  • Actual presentation interaction, navigation, imagery, layout and downloads work at desktop and narrow widths. Print/PDF view is intentional if delivered.
  • Verification was performed on the owner's own devices using private network and workspace details that are intentionally excluded from this public package; only the resulting pass/fail record matters here.
  • No publishing, purchases, outbound outreach or unrelated repo changes. Do not commit; parent integrates and commits scoped changes.
