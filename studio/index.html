    A CONSEQUENTIAL DESIGN CHOICE

    Jurassic Park changes
    the hardware brief.

    The Raw Thrills 2015 game uses mounted potentiometer guns. A pair of handheld USB guns is not the same experience or a proven drop-in interface.

    Keep its original gun/I/O path, or qualify a genuinely compatible mounted-control assembly. Older Sega Jurassic games are separate choices, not hidden substitutions.

    05 / THE BUSINESS CASE

    A product someone can buy.
    A margin that survives delivery.

    The commercial offer must name its exclusions as clearly as its promise. A private arcade and a manufactured game product are different businesses.

      06 / THE PRODUCTION SYSTEM

      Design the tenth unit
      while building the first.

      Buy displays and proven control components. Manufacture the repeatable enclosure, interfaces and service experience around them.

      1. 01

        Release

        Measured hardware, first-article drawings, nesting and assembly instructions.

      2. 02

        Make

        CNC or templated panels, repeatable finish and inspected fit.

      3. 03

        Integrate

        Keyed, labeled harnesses and replaceable control/electronics modules.

      4. 04

        Test

        Serial-level input, restart and endurance checks before packing.

      5. 05

        Deliver

        Packaging and basement access validated with the intended shipping method.

      6. 06

        Support

        Known replacement parts, fault isolation and a funded warranty reserve.

      08 / THE DECISION

      Approve the next proof.
      Not the whole factory.

      Choose the exact four releases, the experience fidelity and a total spending ceiling. Then authorize the smallest prototype that can disprove the expensive assumptions.

      Engineering plan and acceptance criteria ↗
      1. 01

        What must feel authentic?

        Exact game versions, multiplayer mode, mounted versus handheld guns, and the experience elements worth paying for.

      2. 02

        What is the room and budget?

        Measured basement/access route and a ceiling that includes lawful game systems, not only the furniture.

      3. 03

        Is this a personal build or a product pilot?

        Keep prototype spending and commercial tooling/inventory decisions separate. No production order before demand and margin gates pass.
