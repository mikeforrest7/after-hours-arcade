Primary-source evidence that changes the build

Golden Tee and early Big Buck

https://github.com/mamedev/mame/blob/master/src/mame/itech/iteagle.cpp

Inspected current source declares:

  • gtfore06: “Golden Tee Fore! 2006 Complete (v6.00.01)”, MACHINE_SUPPORTS_SAVE; macro year 2005. Marketing edition years and source/copyright years differ.
  • bbh: “Big Buck Hunter (v1.00.14)”, same save flag.
  • bbhsc: “Big Buck Hunter - Shooter's Challenge (v1.60.01)”, same save flag.
  • bbh2sp: “Big Buck Hunter II - Sportsman's Paradise (v2.02.11)”, same save flag.
  • bbhcotw: “Big Buck Hunter Call of the Wild (v3.02.5)”, same save flag.
  • The generic Golden Tee controls include Start, Left, Right, Fly By and Backspin. Do not design a two-button-only panel for Fore!.
  • Big Buck overrides include Trigger and Pump and one gun X/Y pair. Generic shared trackball ports do not prove that a Big Buck cabinet has a trackball or two simultaneous guns.
  • The driver specifies 512 × 384 output and 60 Hz in the common machine configuration. Preserve its aspect ratio on a widescreen display.
  • Fore! and early Big Buck use the appropriate ROM/BIOS/disk image sets. Validate exact installed-build requirements rather than inventing a CHD filename or downloading a mismatched bundle.

Silver Strike donor bill of completeness

https://amusement.itsgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/silver-strike-bowlers-club-2007-international-installation-manual.pdf

Version 10/06, printed pages 3, 7–13 (PDF pages 5, 9–15):

  • Kit includes Nighthawk system box, I/O board, CID, power/video/audio/USB cables, 3-inch trackball and plate, and button/harness parts.
  • System box envelope: 19 W × 7 H × 11 D inches (482.6 × 177.8 × 279.4 mm), excluding required cable and airflow space. This is a 2007-specific dimension, not a universal enclosure promise.
  • CID is a distinct identification component, not synonymous with the I/O board.
  • Buttons: Start, Change Ball, Overhead, Rotate Left, Rotate Right.
  • Video supports selected SVGA 800 × 600, VGA 640 × 480 and lower-resolution modes. A passive VGA-to-HDMI wire is not a signal converter. Prefer a matching-input display or a proven active scaler and measure its latency.
  • Manufacturer explicitly recommends more separation between trackball and monitor to avoid striking the glass. No unsupported numeric “18-inch rule” is asserted.
  • Manufacturer recommends a qualified electrician for required grounded AC wiring. Do not reuse unsafe cabinet mains merely because the game boots.

The correct purchase question is “does this complete, transferable system cold-boot, identify its I/O and CID, pass control tests and finish ten frames?” not “does this disk contain Silver Strike?” Require a live demonstration before accepting a donor. Check its exact version, local-play mode, disk health, cooling and availability of replacement security/identity components. Avoid speculative OS and DRM explanations where no source establishes them.

Software-rights and loader boundary

https://docs.mamedev.org/usingmame/commonissues.html#how-do-i-legally-obtain-roms-or-disk-images-to-run-on-mame

MAME's FAQ distinguishes authorized licensing, explicitly released free media and reading one's actual arcade hardware. It is not a blanket legal opinion for every country, contract or copy-protection scheme. Buying a cabinet does not establish permission to download somebody else's image, redistribute assets or defeat authentication. Do not infer “abandonware” means unprotected.

TeknoParrot and similar compatibility loaders are separate from an all-open-source MAME/Supermodel stack. This plan makes no current exact-title compatibility or license claim for them: verify individual components, supported build, fees, media rights and behavior before considering that route. A loader listing alone does not satisfy the bench gate. No cracked images, activation patches, shared keys or dongle emulation are included.

Required per-game proof before spending on cabinetry

  • Record exact title/build and which original features matter: local versus online play, alternating versus simultaneous players, courses, artwork, sound, mounted-gun feel, recoil and motion.
  • Show authorized cold boot, title screen and full gameplay, not an attract-mode video.
  • Golden Tee: complete 18 holes; test gentle putts and hard drives for trackball saturation, reverse axes, missed counts and hand clearance.
  • Silver Strike: complete ten frames with spares/strikes/foul or gutter cases and all five panel controls. Prove the selected donor's local-play state with network disconnected if offline use is required.
  • Big Buck: test trigger/pump event ordering, edge targets, fast sweeps, and the actual selected multiplayer mode. A one-gun alternating game is not a two-player shootout.
  • Jurassic: independently aim/fire both players, test secondary actions and a complete mission; check calibration after restart. For 2015, confirm original PC/dongle/I/O pairing and define whether recoil is deliberately omitted or safely retained.
  • Record emulator/runtime version, PC, controller firmware, display mode, configuration hashes and observations. Repeat after hardware, display size, firmware or software changes.
